# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want NetRecon to be a respectful, professional, and inclusive project.
Contributors and maintainers are expected to collaborate constructively and prioritize user safety.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional in all project spaces
- Give technical feedback clearly and in good faith
- Assume positive intent while reviewing contributions
- Focus discussions on code, security, and project quality
- Respect legal and ethical boundaries in security topics

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation
- Personal attacks, insults, or inflammatory language
- Sharing private information without consent
- Encouraging illegal or unethical use of this project
- Deliberate disruption of project workflows

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject content and participation that violates this policy.
Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.

## Reporting Conduct Issues

Report concerns to: `voltsparx@gmail.com`

Include relevant context, links, and timestamps if possible.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
